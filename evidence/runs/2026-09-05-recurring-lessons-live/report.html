Why do recorded lessons in an agentic engineering system recur without ever binding?

The observations provisionally support that recorded lessons recur without binding when collection and recall are not transformed into deterministic checks. Promotion machinery exists but remains unfed; transforming lessons may contribute to interrupting recurrence, but the exact transformation and blocking mechanism remain unresolved.
